DONBEX Radio App V26.4 — PayPal clean flow

- PayPal attempts are NO LONGER written to radioRequests before payment.
- Therefore unpaid/cancelled PayPal attempts do not appear in DONBEX Admin as manual approvals.
- The client only uploads the logo needed for the pending checkout.
- The protected server creates a paypalSessions document, creates/captures the PayPal order, verifies amount/status, and only then writes the final radioRequests document as APPROVED and activates the radio.
- Bank transfer flow is unchanged: it still creates pending_approval and requires Admin review.
- PayPal remains embedded via PayPal JS SDK and opens the private tracking panel only after confirmed capture.
